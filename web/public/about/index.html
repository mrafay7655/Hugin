01The problem

Scammers move in minutes. Banks answer in hours.

When someone gets a call asking for their one-time code, the next few minutes decide whether they lose their savings. Today they message the bank and join a queue. An AI could answer instantly, but no bank can safely let one.

The question we set out to answer

How can a bank let an AI act in the first minutes of a scam, without it ever doing something the bank cannot defend?

Victims ask where banks aren't looking

03

The public square is unwatched

Scam giveaways and impersonators post under the bank's own posts. Angry and sarcastic comments sit untriaged. Nobody can say how customers feel about a campaign until it is too late.

"You've won $500, claim now at ravenw00d-rewards.com" · under the bank's own launch post, visible to every customer
04

A plain chatbot is a liability

An unconstrained model can be talked into anything. In a bank, one wrong sentence is an incident.

Asks for a PIN · promises a refund · links to a lookalike site · invents a fee · is tricked by "ignore your rules"
05

Banks must prove every decision

Regulators and risk teams ask who decided, on what source, and why. "The AI said so" is not an answer.

03Conversations

A scam report, handled in seconds. A fee question, answered with a source.

  • Understands real messages. Plain text, a voice note or a screenshot of the scam text, in the customer's own words.
  • Acts on a clear fraud report. Locks the card, opens a case, alerts the Fraud team and reassures the customer. Only a person can unlock.
  • Spots lookalike links. "ravenw00d-verify.com" is not "ravenwoodbank.com". Every domain is compared to the bank's own.
  • Answers from approved policy with the section cited, or says it has no source and hands over. It never guesses.
  • Hands risky cases to people. Account changes and refunds become a drafted reply a person approves. Nothing is sent without them.

05Comments & moderation

The bank's public pages, moderated.

Scammers do not only message customers. They post under the bank's own posts, where every customer can see them. HUGIN reads each comment and puts a person's attention where it matters first.

  • Scored on four things. Sentiment, sarcasm, topic and risk. "Love waiting for my paycheck 🙃" is read as negative, not praise.
  • Risky first. The queue starts with scams, fraud reports and legal threats, then what needs a reply.
  • Scams get hidden, not answered. A reply to a scam only gives it reach, so none is drafted.
  • Questions get a sourced reply. Drafted from approved policy with the section cited. Fraud reports and complaints move to a private message.
  • The same guard. A public reply that asks for an account number or PIN is blocked, and every action is audited.

08Why a bank can trust it

The model proposes. Code decides.

HUGIN uses Gemini for what it is best at, understanding messy human language. Everything that can hurt someone is handled by small, testable code the model cannot override.

Gemini understands

Reads text, voice notes and screenshots, in any wording. Returns a structured reading: what the customer wants, how they feel, how sure it is.

Google ADK · Gemini 2.5 Flash · read-only tools

Rules decide

A deterministic policy engine chooses what happens. A clear fraud report locks the card at once. A vague keyword goes to a person, never straight to action.

8 policies · a strong rule hit cannot be downgraded by the model

Guards check every word

Every outgoing message, from the agent or a person, passes one gate. No request for a PIN or code, no refund promise, no link to anywhere but the bank.

One exit for every message · blocked attempts are audited

13Honest limits

What this is, and isn't yet.

Telegram is the open channel

WhatsApp is built and tested, but Meta's test number only reaches five registered phones, so the bot you can try is on Telegram.

Comments are sample data here

Instagram and Facebook comment handling is built and tested against Meta's format, but no live Page is connected. The comments you see are seeded examples and the ones you post.

It takes a few seconds

A typical message is answered in roughly 2 to 10 seconds. Gemini's speed varies, and the first message after a quiet spell is slower.

Retrieval is simple on purpose

Keyword search over approved documents, conservative by design: below a relevance score it says it has no source instead of guessing.

The bank is fictional

Ravenwood Bank, its customers and its numbers are made up. Sample data is labelled SAMPLE throughout.
